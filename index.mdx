# The Citizen's Record

**A record people can inspect for themselves.**

The Citizen's Record brings together public documents, civic research, source methodology, and practical tools for understanding how records are created and what they actually establish.

We separate allegations from findings, filings from outcomes, and evidence from interpretation.
