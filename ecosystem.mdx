# Project Ecosystem

The Citizen's Record ecosystem is a set of related projects with different jobs. The goal is to share standards and research practices without turning every project into the same application.

## The Citizen's Record

The public-facing civic record. It presents documented records, investigations, official sources, filings, outcomes, and research context.

[Open the main site](https://thomascallen16.github.io/The-Citizens-Record/)

## Documentation Hub

This site is the knowledge and methodology layer. It explains how the projects work, how sources are evaluated, and how people can research public information responsibly.

## Pro Se Compass

A research and case-organization product intended to help people structure records, timelines, evidence, questions, filings, and deadlines. It is organizational and research support, not a substitute for legal advice.

## ProofFlow

An evidence workflow focused on traceability: connecting source material to chronology, claims, contradictions, missing evidence, and verification.

## Shared operating principles

All projects should favor:

- source traceability;
- explicit uncertainty;
- reproducible workflows;
- clear separation between facts and allegations;
- privacy and data integrity;
- small, verifiable changes; and
- documentation that explains real behavior rather than marketing claims.

## Architecture principle

Keep projects separate when separation improves reliability, privacy, deployment, or user understanding. Connect them through documented interfaces, links, shared standards, and source references rather than unnecessary coupling.
