# Start Here

The Citizen's Record is designed for readers who want to go beyond headlines and examine the underlying record.

## Begin with the question

Write down exactly what you are trying to establish. Avoid starting with a conclusion and searching only for material that confirms it.

## Find the record

Look for the most direct available source: an official document, docket entry, filing, order, dataset, recording, or other primary material.

## Identify the status

Ask what the document actually is:

- An allegation?
- A request?
- A response?
- A filing?
- An order?
- A finding?
- A final outcome?

## Preserve the trail

Keep copies of the source and record where it came from. Note relevant dates, identifiers, and links.

## Read the methodology

Before relying on a published record, review our [Methodology](/methodology) so you understand how claims and corrections are handled.

<Note>
The Citizen's Record provides information and research resources. It does not replace an attorney, official records custodian, or other qualified professional.
</Note>
