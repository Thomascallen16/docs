# Legal Information and Research

Legal research begins with identifying exactly what happened procedurally and what authority governs it.

## Separate the layers

Keep these categories distinct:

- Facts and evidence
- Court docket entries
- Filed allegations and responses
- Orders and judgments
- Statutes and regulations
- Court rules
- Judicial opinions

## Read the actual language

Summaries are useful starting points, but the operative text matters. A statute may contain definitions, exceptions, deadlines, or cross-references that change the analysis.

## Track versions and dates

Law changes. Always note the version and effective date relevant to the event or proceeding you are researching.

## Not legal advice

The Citizen's Record may provide research tools and educational information, but individual legal strategy should be discussed with a qualified attorney when possible.
