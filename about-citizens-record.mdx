# What is The Citizen's Record?

The Citizen's Record is a public-interest project built to make important civic information easier to find, inspect, and understand.

Our purpose is not to tell people what to think. Our purpose is to build a record people can examine for themselves.

## What we publish

- Public records and official documents
- Docket and filing timelines
- Source-based investigations
- Requests for government records
- Civic and legal research guides
- Tools that help people organize information

## The standard

Whenever possible, important factual claims should lead back to a primary or authoritative source. We distinguish between allegations, filings, responses, findings, and outcomes.

A record can change. When it does, the documentation should show what changed and why.

> **Important:** The Citizen's Record provides information and research tools. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
