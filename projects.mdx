# Projects and Tools

The Citizen's Record is the public-facing record and knowledge hub. Additional projects can provide specialized workflows without mixing every function into one product.

## Citizen's Record

The public record, research hub, methodology, and source-based civic information.

## Pro Se Compass

A planned organizational and research workspace for people navigating legal matters without an attorney. It is intended to help users organize information, timelines, documents, and research—not replace professional legal advice.

## ProofFlow

A planned evidence and workflow project focused on organizing materials into traceable, reviewable structures.

As these products mature, each can receive its own dedicated documentation while remaining connected through the Citizen's Record ecosystem.
